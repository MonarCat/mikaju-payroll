Before you contact us

Include your app version (shown in Settings → About), operating system, and the country your company is configured for — this lets us resolve most issues in the first reply.

Billing & subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed in USD via Paystack, with the local currency equivalent shown at checkout. To change plans, update payment details, or cancel, sign in on the website or in the app and go to Account → Billing. Cancelling stops future billing immediately; your data remains accessible at Free-tier limits per our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, email security@mikajupayroll.com directly rather than filing a public issue. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours.

Common questions

My installer won't run on Windows 7 or 8.

Use the dedicated legacy build — see Troubleshooting.

I'm not seeing recent changes on a second device.

Cloud sync requires an active internet connection and a Basic or Enterprise plan. Check Settings → Sync Status on both devices.

How do I request my data or delete my account?

Email privacy@mikajupayroll.com. See your rights in full under the Privacy Policy.
