1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Mikaju Software Solutions ("Mikaju," "we," "us") handles personal data in connection with the Mikaju Payroll website and desktop application (the "Service"). It applies to: (a) account data of the person or business using Mikaju ("Customer"), and (b) employee personal data that a Customer enters into the Service to run payroll ("Employee Data").

Controller vs. processor.For Customer account data (billing, login, support), Mikaju acts as the data controller. For Employee Data entered by a Customer to run its own payroll, the Customer is the data controller and Mikaju acts as a data processor acting only on the Customer's instructions. See our Data Processing terms for the processor relationship in detail.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases, consistent with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and equivalent international frameworks:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Service you've signed up for, including payroll calculation and cloud sync
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the Service, prevent fraud, and improve reliability
  • Legal obligation — where required to comply with applicable law
  • Consent — where we ask separately, such as for optional marketing communications

4. How We Use Data

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Service, including offline-first calculation and cloud sync
  • To process payments via Paystack and manage your subscription
  • To provide support and respond to your requests
  • To send essential service communications (billing, security, statutory updates relevant to your country)
  • To improve the Service through aggregated, de-identified usage insights

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Employee Data for advertising.

5. Sharing & Subprocessors

We do not share Employee Data with third parties for their own independent purposes. We may disclose data where required by law, a valid court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Mikaju, our Customers, or others.

6. International Transfers

Mikaju operates from Kenya and serves Customers across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Burundi, South Sudan, Somalia, and DR Congo. Cloud-synced data may be stored or processed on infrastructure located outside your country, including outside Africa, via our subprocessors listed above. Where we transfer personal data across borders, we rely on contractual safeguards with our subprocessors and take reasonable steps to ensure a comparable level of protection to that required under Kenyan and applicable regional data protection law.

7. Retention

We retain account and Employee Data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and audit obligations (statutory payroll records are commonly subject to multi-year retention requirements under East African labor and tax law, which Customers should account for independently). On request, we will delete or anonymize personal data that is no longer required, except where retention is legally mandated.

8. Security

Data is encrypted locally on your device and encrypted in transit and at rest when synced to the cloud. Access to production systems is restricted and logged. No system is completely secure; if we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected Customers and, where required, the relevant regulator without undue delay.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and, where applicable, GDPR-equivalent rights), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) or the equivalent authority in your country

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@mikajupayroll.com. If you are an employee whose data was entered by your employer (the Customer), please contact your employer directly, as they control that data; we will support them in fulfilling your request.

10. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies required for sign-in and checkout, and may use limited analytics cookies to understand aggregate site usage. The desktop app does not use advertising trackers. Where required, we will request consent for non-essential cookies via a website banner.

11. Children's Data

The Service is intended for business use by adults managing payroll. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Employee Data entered by a Customer should relate only to individuals of legal working age under applicable local law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice before taking effect.

13. Contact & Data Protection Officer

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@mikajupayroll.com. Mikaju Software Solutions is based in Nairobi, Kenya.
