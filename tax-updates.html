Tax Updates

A dated record of statutory rate and rule changes across all 9 supported countries, and how each was reflected in Mikaju's tax engine. Every update ships automatically at no extra cost.

Tax engine version tracked separately from app version · See Changelog for app releases
How this works.Mikaju's tax engine uses versioned rate tables per country. When a government publishes a new PAYE band, social security rate, or levy, we update the relevant table and ship it in the next release. Payroll runs you've already locked keep the rates that were in effect at approval time, so historical payslips and certificates stay accurate even after a rate change.

Kenya (KE)

Full engineCurrent

PAYE bands, NSSF Tier I/II contributions, SHIF, Housing Levy, and P9A annual certificate generation are fully modeled and kept current with KRA and NSSF published rates.

Uganda (UG)

Full engineCurrent

PAYE, NSSF (5% employee + 10% employer), and Local Service Tax are fully modeled against URA and NSSF Uganda published rates.

Tanzania (TZ)

Full engineCurrent

PAYE, NSSF (10% employee + 10% employer), Skills Development Levy (SDL), and Workers Compensation Fund (WCF) contributions are fully modeled against TRA published rates.

Rwanda (RW)

Full engineCurrent

PAYE, RSSB Pension, and RSSB Health Insurance contributions are fully modeled against RRA and RSSB published rates.

Ethiopia (ET)

Full engineCurrent

PAYE and pension contributions (private and public sector rates) are fully modeled against Ethiopian Revenue and Customs Authority (later ERCA/MoR) published rates.

Burundi (BI)

Core engineCurrent

PAYE and INSS contributions (6% employee + 15% employer) are modeled. Additional statutory detail is being expanded toward full-engine coverage.

South Sudan (SS)

Core engineCurrent

PAYE and SSPS contributions (8% employee + 17% employer) are modeled. Additional statutory detail is being expanded toward full-engine coverage.

Somalia (SO)

Core engineCurrent

Income tax and social contribution calculations are modeled at core level. Additional statutory detail is being expanded toward full-engine coverage.

DR Congo (CD)

Core engineCurrent

IPR (income tax), INSS contributions (5% employee + 13% employer), and ONEM are modeled at core level. Additional statutory detail is being expanded toward full-engine coverage.

Subscribe to updates

Statutory rate changes are shipped automatically through the app's regular update channel — there is nothing to configure. If you'd like to be notified by email ahead of a change taking effect, contact Support.
