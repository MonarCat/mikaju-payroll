Core Capabilities

Everything your HR team needs

From a single employee to hundreds across the region. Mikaju handles every calculation, certificate, and compliance requirement — automatically.

Employee Management

Full register: national ID, tax PIN, social security numbers, bank details, and country-specific required fields for all 9 nations.

Payroll Run & Approval

Select a period, auto-calculate gross, net and all statutory deductions. Review, approve and lock payroll runs in seconds.

Compliant Payslips

A4 PDF payslips in the correct statutory format for your country. Bulk-export an entire payroll run in one click.

Tax Certificate Generator

P9A for Kenya; equivalent annual certificates for Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and beyond. Exact formats for employee filings.

Payroll Import

Bulk employee data or monthly payroll via CSV or Excel. Intelligent column mapping handles messy source files.

Cloud Sync & Backup

Encrypted local database syncs to secure cloud automatically when online. Multi-device access on paid plans.

Offline-First Architecture
