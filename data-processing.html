Data Processing Terms

These terms describe how Mikaju Software Solutions processes Employee Data on behalf of Customers who use Mikaju Payroll to run their own payroll, consistent with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and GDPR-equivalent processor obligations.

These Data Processing Terms ("DPT") apply whenever a Customer ("you," "Controller") uses Mikaju Payroll to process personal data of its own employees ("Employee Data"). They supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Where these DPT conflict with the Terms of Service on data processing matters, these DPT govern.

1. Roles of the Parties

For Employee Data, you (the Customer) are the data controller — you determine what employee data is collected and why. Mikaju Software Solutions is the data processor — we process Employee Data solely to provide the Service and solely on your documented instructions, as set out in these terms and your use of the Service's features.

2. Subject Matter & Duration

The subject matter is the provision of offline-first payroll and HR software, including payroll calculation, payslip and tax certificate generation, employee record management, and optional cloud sync and backup. Processing continues for as long as your account is active and data remains stored per Section 12.

3. Nature & Purpose of Processing

Employee Data is processed to: calculate statutory payroll deductions and net pay; generate payslips and annual tax certificates; maintain an employee register; back up and, where enabled, sync data to the cloud for multi-device access; and provide support at your request.

4. Categories of Data & Data Subjects

Data subjects: employees, contractors, and other individuals whose payroll you process using the Service.

Data categories: name, national ID number, tax PIN/TIN, social security or pension number, bank account details, salary and compensation data, employment dates and terms, and any additional country-specific statutory fields you enter.

5. Processing on Documented Instructions

We process Employee Data only on your instructions, as expressed through your configuration and use of the Service (for example, the country you select, the employees you add, and the payroll runs you approve). We will not process Employee Data for any other purpose, including our own marketing, without your separate, explicit consent. If we believe an instruction violates applicable data protection law, we will notify you before carrying it out.

6. Confidentiality

We ensure that personnel authorized to process Employee Data are bound by confidentiality obligations, whether contractual or statutory, and access Employee Data only to the extent necessary to provide the Service or support you've requested.

7. Security Measures

We maintain technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Local encryption of the on-device database and encryption in transit and at rest for synced data
  • Row-level access controls and authenticated access to cloud infrastructure
  • Restricted, logged access to production systems by authorized personnel only
  • Regular review of dependencies and infrastructure for known vulnerabilities

9. Assisting the Controller

Taking into account the nature of processing, we will reasonably assist you in responding to data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) relating to Employee Data, and in meeting your obligations around security, breach notification, and data protection impact assessments, insofar as this relates to our processing under the Service.

10. Breach Notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting Employee Data, we will notify you without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware, with the information reasonably available to us at that time, so that you can meet your own notification obligations as controller.

11. International Transfers

Where Employee Data is transferred outside your country as part of cloud sync via our subprocessors, we rely on contractual safeguards with those subprocessors and take reasonable steps to ensure protection consistent with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and comparable international standards. If you are subject to additional cross-border transfer restrictions under your local law, contact us to discuss the appropriate safeguards for your account.

12. Return & Deletion of Data

On termination of your account, or on your written request, we will delete or return Employee Data within a reasonable period, except where we are required by law to retain it. Local, on-device data remains under your control and can be exported or deleted directly within the app at any time.

13. Audit Rights

On reasonable prior written notice, and no more than once per year (except following a confirmed breach), Enterprise plan Customers may request evidence of our compliance with this DPT, which we will provide in the form of relevant documentation, certifications, or a summary of our security practices. On-site audits may be arranged by mutual agreement for good cause.

14. Liability

Liability under these Data Processing Terms is subject to the limitation of liability provisions in our Terms of Service.
